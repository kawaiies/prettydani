Find Your Insole with the CURREX® Insole Finder

Need help finding which CURREX insole is right for you? Answer six quick questions to find your perfect match.

CURREX dynamic insoles were specifically engineered for the unique demands of individual sports. Our insole finder compiles years of research into six simple questions to help you find the right insoles.

During the insole finder quiz, you will choose your sport and activity, tell us about your foot type and leg axis. We'll match you with the perfect CURREX insole for your needs.
