CURREX x Alex Freeman: Powering the Next Generation of Speed & Precision

CURREX is proud to announce a new partnership with Alex Freeman, one of the brightest young talents in professional soccer. A dynamic defender known for his speed, composure, and attacking instincts, Alex represents the modern evolution of the game: athletic, versatile, and fearless in transition. As his professional career continues to gain momentum, CURREX is excited to support an athlete whose trajectory reflects dedication, growth, and high-level performance.

A Career Defined by Progression and Potential

Alex Freeman’s professional journey has already spanned elite competition on both domestic and international stages. After developing within the Orlando City SC system, he emerged as a standout in Major League Soccer (MLS), earning MLS Young Player of the Year honors in 2025 and a selection as an MLS All-Star. Exposure to different playing styles and tactical systems accelerated his development and broadened his impact on the field.

He currently competes at a high level in Spain’s La Liga with Villarreal and continues to demonstrate the qualities that define the modern fullback: explosive recovery speed, confident overlapping runs, and the ability to influence both defensive structure and attacking transitions.

Making His Mark with the USMNT

Alex’s impressive club performances have translated to the international stage as well. His early appearances with the United States Men’s National Team (USMNT) signal a promising international future. His rapid integration into the national team setup reflects not just talent, but consistency and trust earned through performance.

Thriving in the Modern Game

Today’s professional soccer landscape is faster and more demanding than ever. Fullbacks and defenders must combine sprint speed with tactical awareness, stamina with sharp technical ability. Every match includes explosive runs, rapid changes of direction, and sustained defensive concentration.

Alex Freeman’s game reflects that evolution. His quick recovery speed allows him to neutralize counterattacks. His balance and control help him maintain possession under pressure. His work rate ensures he impacts both ends of the field.

These qualities don’t happen by accident. They are built through disciplined training, attention to detail, and a foundation that allows repeatable, efficient movement over 90-plus minutes.

How CLEATPRO™ Complements Alex’s Game

At CURREX, we engineer insoles specifically for the demands of cleated sports. Soccer combines acceleration, lateral shifts, torque, and repetitive impact all within a tight-fitting cleat. For Alex, precision foot placement and confident movement are essential components of his performance.

"CURREX® CLEATPRO™ keeps up with the demands of the game, day in and day out. When I’m at my best with the ball at my feet, I can step on the field confident and focused on doing my job." - Alex Freeman

CURREX CLEATPRO™ features Dynamic Arch Technology™ (DAT), offering arch-specific profiles (High, Medium, or Low) designed to support natural movement while enhancing alignment and control. By improving internal stability within the cleat, the insole helps athletes maintain consistent footwork during high-speed transitions and physical challenges.

A Shared Drive for Excellence

Alex’s career reflects steady progression, resilience, and a commitment to becoming better every season. That pursuit of excellence mirrors CURREX’s philosophy of building sport-specific insoles that enhance performance from the ground up.

This partnership is rooted in a shared understanding: success is built on preparation, repetition, and a strong foundation. As Alex Freeman continues to establish himself on the professional stage, CURREX is proud to stand behind him, supporting the movement, confidence, and precision that define his game.
